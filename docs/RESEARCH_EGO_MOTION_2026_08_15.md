# GMC-Link Ego/Background Motion — Final Research Report (2026-08-15)

## 1. LANDSCAPE

Production MOT ego-compensation has converged on exactly the family we ship: sparse keypoints + robust low-DOF fit (BoT-SORT's GMC defaults to goodFeaturesToTrack + pyramidal LK + `estimateAffinePartial2D`, having tried ORB/SIFT/ECC variants — [arXiv:2206.14651](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.14651), [Ultralytics gmc.py](https://docs.ultralytics.com/reference/trackers/utils/gmc)), with the research edge moving in two directions that both abandon the image plane: ground-plane compensation with camera intrinsics (UCMCTrack, AAAI 2024, [arXiv:2312.08952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08952)) and ego-pose/depth decoupling (EMAP, [arXiv:2404.03110](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03110)) — i.e., the field's own answer to "better ego" is depth or odometry, not a better 2D warp. Learned components fit our budget only at the margins (XFeat/LighterGlue, [arXiv:2404.19174](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.19174)) and are documented to be *worse* than classical detectors at sub-pixel localization (Keypt2Subpx, [ECCV 2024](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/10082.pdf)); zero-shot dense flow is 4× worse than our median (SEA-RAFT KITTI Fl-epe 3.62px, [arXiv:2405.14793](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.14793)). Our ORB1500 + similarity-4DOF + composition pipeline sits at the measured Pareto frontier of this entire classical family (A12: 8 detector/budget/estimator configs, none beat 0.896–0.898px/15–18ms; A13: similarity kills the wild tail by construction, 0/916), and the A14 referee (2026-08-15, GT-static-track residuals) resolved the last open structural question — composition beats direct wide-baseline estimation at every gap on every sequence, and the binding error is depth parallax at 3–57px against a 0.9px estimator residual, which no global 2D transform of any DOF can fix (RESEARCH_NOTES.md §A14, "EGO STAGE CLOSED").

## 3. DEAD ENDS

- **Direct per-gap wide-baseline estimation** (proposed independently in all 4 areas): A14 GT-static referee — composed WINS every gap/seq (0005 gap-10: 57.3 vs 80.5px); direct dies to depth parallax, not matching (0/176 failures); TD-2 rationale inverted.
- **Keyframe/anchor chaining (K=3–5):** accumulation hypothesis falsified by the same referee; explicitly named dead in A14; anchor baselines take a milder dose of the same parallax poison.
- **Windowed sim(2) Lie synchronization** (Govindu CVPR 2004 / TRGMC-lite): its added measurements are exactly the parallax-corrupted direct estimates; inlier weights are blind to the defect; no direct-failure frames exist to rescue.
- **Kalman/precision-weighted smoothing on the transform stream** (sim2-cv-kf, uncertainty-weighted composition): "Kalman-on-H" named dead in A14; random-walk variance is not the error budget; wild tail already 0/916 by construction (A13); adds a q/r hyperparameter against the simplicity rule.
- **LK sparse-flow GMC** (BoT-SORT default mechanism): sub-pixel median play against a 3–57px parallax wall — two orders below the binding error; stage closed; speed headroom moot at 14.5ms.
- **ECC photometric polish** ([Evangelidis & Psarakis TPAMI 2008](https://learnopencv.com/image-alignment-ecc-in-opencv-c-python/)): refines a global 2D transform, which A14 proved cannot touch the binding error; bounded by oxts +0.285; anti-aligned with the professor-directed simplification days after it shipped.
- **Keypt2Subpx learned sub-pixel refinement** (ECCV 2024): median axis ceiling-killed twice over (0.045px gain vs ±0.35 MOVING seed noise); only the free classical control survives, as L1 documentation.
- **XFeat + MNN / LighterGlue matcher upgrades** ([arXiv:2404.19174](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.19174)): escalations of the dead direct-per-gap axis; correspondence quality was never the failure mode; would add the ego stage's first GPU dependency to a closed stage.
- **SEA-RAFT masked dense flow → 4-DOF fit:** entry condition (sparse inlier starvation at gap-10) measured to never fire; dense-flow family graveyarded (Farneback worst-of-12, RAFT lost at design); 3.62px zero-shot EPE vs 0.9px classical.
- **Road-plane anchored ego flow** (plane+parallax / UCMCTrack-inspired): only candidate on the right error axis, but bounded above by the closed Path-B perfect-information result (+0.285 pooled, iKUN-only, FH NEG); 12D uses centroids ~0.8m above the plane so parallax survives even ideally; texture-poor asphalt feasibility risk. Survives only as rebuttal ammo (L2).

## 4. HONEST CEILING NOTE

Three measurements pin the ceiling of *everything* in this report. (1) The entire ego-compensation component is worth +2.12 MOVING HOTA on iKUN only (ablation n=5, p<1e-3; ego is NEG on FH — project_fh_ego_lean_v2_negative). (2) Replacing our estimator with GT-grade ego (KITTI oxts GPS+IMU, Path B) gained only +0.285 pooled on that one host — that is the hard upper bound on ALL estimator-accuracy improvements, achieved with perfect information we're not allowed to use. (3) A14's parallax wall: the estimator's 0.9px inlier residual sits two orders of magnitude below the 3–57px object-level depth-parallax error, and no global 2D transform of any DOF closes that gap — the only fixes (depth, ground plane, odometry) either break frames-only or are already bounded by (2). With MOVING seed noise at ±0.35 (n=3), no surviving lever except possibly D2 can produce a resolvable HOTA effect, and D2's pre-registered ceiling is +0.285. **Stop rule:** run D1 as a correctness rider (null = success), run D2's cheap pre-screen once with user sign-off; if D2's 12D deltas are sub-threshold or its HOTA lands inside noise, the ego stage is finished — permanently — and remaining effort belongs to the aligner/fusion side, where the audit already shows the open error budget. The A12/A13/A14 tables are the paper's "why not a stronger ego estimator" defense; that is their remaining value.
